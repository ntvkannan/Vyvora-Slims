Our Philosophy

“Ancient Wisdom.
Modern You.”

We believe true wellness is achieved at the intersection of time-honored holistic understanding and precise, modern clinical practices. Founder Story

Mr. Ilayaraj

Founder

I started my career in 2000 at VLCC, working part-time for two years. During this time, I learned about slimming and gained an understanding of weight-loss therapies.

I then began working full-time with a start-up company, where I gained valuable experience and knowledge in Ayurvedic treatments, slimming treatments, marketing, counselling, and sales.

Later, I joined another start-up company as a Manager, where I gained hands-on experience managing the centre and providing services directly to clients.

Recognising my dedication and service, one of my clients, Mr. Selvaraj, sponsored me with a G5 machine and encouraged me to explore home services, freelancing, and independent practice.

This experience eventually led me to open Trimme in HSR Layout as a working partner, marking an important step in my journey in the slimming and wellness field.

Today, VYVORA SLIMS continues this journey with a clear focus on helping individuals achieve their weight-management goals through personalised slimming and wellness solutions, tailored to their individual needs and lifestyle.

Mission & Vision

Mission

Weight-loss is a mental thing. Mission Slim Possible is our tool to getting diet revenge in ten weeks. Did you ever wonder why slim people are not on a diet? It’s because diets don’t work. It’s time you discover how slim people think, act and eat. Slim is possible for you without dieting! We will help you set realistic attainable Daily or Weekly, step by step action plans that empower and motivate you towards your weight loss target. Help you manage your weight by focusing on what you “can do” not on what you “can’t” do. Give you that extra push, focus and reassurance that you can do what you set out to do. We Provide you with usable guidance.

Vision
