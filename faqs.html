Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about weight loss, inch loss, slimming and body-contouring treatments at VYVORA SLIMS.

General

Unlike a gym or generic diet clinic, VYVORA SLIMS takes a personalized, technology-led approach to slimming and body contouring. Every client begins with a detailed consultation to understand their goals, body type and lifestyle before any treatment option is discussed. Rather than following a single fixed program, we offer a range of advanced, non-invasive treatment technologies that can be tailored to individual requirements, with progress reviewed and adjusted along the way.

Client safety is a priority at every stage. Our treatment options use established, non-invasive technologies and are administered by trained professionals following standard safety protocols. Suitability for any treatment is assessed individually during your consultation, taking into account your health history and goals, so that the most appropriate option is recommended for you.

Yes. A consultation is required before any treatment begins. This helps our team understand your goals, assess your body's individual requirements, and discuss which treatment options are best suited to you. It also gives you the opportunity to ask questions and set realistic expectations before committing to a program.

Weight Loss & Inch Loss

Results vary from person to person depending on factors such as body type, lifestyle, consistency and the treatment option chosen. Some clients notice initial changes within the first few sessions, while more visible results typically build up over a series of sessions. Your consultation will give you a realistic, personalized timeline based on your specific goals.

Long-term results depend largely on maintaining a healthy lifestyle after your program is complete. Our team provides guidance on diet, activity and habits to help you sustain your results. As with any weight-management approach, significant changes in diet or activity levels after the program can affect long-term outcomes.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all restrictive diets. Instead, our team recommends realistic, sustainable dietary guidance based on your goals, routine and treatment plan. Following these recommendations consistently helps support and enhance the results of your treatment.

Body Contouring

Most of our body contouring treatment options are non-invasive and generally well-tolerated. Clients may experience mild sensations such as warmth, pressure or a cooling effect depending on the technology used, but significant pain is not typical. Your comfort is discussed and monitored throughout each session.

Body contouring is designed to target and shape specific areas of the body rather than achieve overall weight reduction. It works best as a complement to a healthy lifestyle or weight-loss program, particularly for areas that don't respond as easily to diet and exercise alone. During your consultation, we'll discuss whether body contouring, weight loss, or a combination of both is right for your goals.

The number of sessions required depends on the treatment option selected, the area being treated, and your individual goals. Some clients see visible changes after a few sessions, while others may need a longer series for their desired outcome. A personalized session plan is recommended after your consultation and assessment.

Consultation & Treatment

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothing is recommended for your consultation, as this may involve a brief physical assessment of the treatment area. There's no need for any special preparation — our team will guide you through each step and explain what to expect at every stage.

Most of our treatment options are non-invasive with little to no downtime, allowing you to resume your daily activities soon after a session. Some clients may notice mild, temporary redness or sensitivity in the treated area. Any specific aftercare instructions relevant to your treatment will be shared by our team.
